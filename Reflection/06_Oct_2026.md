## Detailed Notes

### 1. Mid semester Project Presentations
* **Culmination of Work:** Today's session was entirely dedicated to the project presentations. This marked the culmination of weeks of planning, coding, and refining, providing a platform for every group to showcase their completed software applications to the faculty and the rest of the class.

### 2. Presenting Our Visualizer Application
* **Showcasing Our Work:** Our group (Group 3) presented our interactive Binary Tree and BST visualizer. We demonstrated the core functionality, including real-time tree generation, the responsiveness of our JavaFX UI controls, and the seamless handling of our mathematical layout algorithms.

### 3. Observing and Learning from Peer Presentations
* **Broadening Perspectives:** Watching the other groups present their projects was highly educational. It provided insight into completely different approaches to similar programming problems, varying architectural styles, and unique UI/UX design philosophies.
* **Constructive Feedback:** Listening to the faculty's questions and feedback on other projects reinforced our previous lessons—there is immense value in absorbing the critiques given to peers. It highlights industry best practices and exposes common pitfalls to avoid in future development cycles.

## Key Takeaways

* **Continuous Learning:** Observing peer presentations is a fantastic opportunity to broaden your technical perspective, learn alternative problem-solving approaches, and absorb widespread constructive feedback.
